# Contributing to SI-Agents

## Before contributing

- Read `AGENTS.md` and `SIA_SPECS.md`.
- Keep changes evidence-driven and independently authored.
- Do not submit copied prompts, persona text, documentation, code, or assets from another project unless the license and provenance have been explicitly reviewed and allow the use.
- Do not add secrets, private source, credentials, confidential material, or paid-only proprietary content.
- Review dependency licenses before introducing new dependencies.
- OpenCode and OmniRoute may be referenced when implementing their actual SI-Agents integration boundaries.

## Provenance attestation

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that:

1. you created the contribution yourself or have the necessary rights to submit it;
2. the contribution does not knowingly include unauthorized third-party copyrighted material;
3. any third-party material included in the contribution is identified and licensed for the intended use;
4. you are not submitting confidential or restricted material that you are not authorized to disclose;
5. you will disclose known provenance or licensing concerns before merge.

## Commit sign-off

Contributors should sign off commits with:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

The sign-off is an attestation of contribution provenance; it is not a substitute for legal review of unusual or externally sourced material.

## Review expectations

Pull requests should include:

- a concise problem statement;
- implementation and root-cause evidence where applicable;
- tests and verification results;
- documentation updates when behavior or architecture changes;
- dependency/license impact;
- provenance notes for externally sourced material.

## Legal concerns

If a copyright, trademark, patent, license, ownership, employment, contractor, confidentiality, or DMCA concern is raised, pause distribution of the affected material and escalate for qualified legal review. Do not delete or rewrite evidence to conceal provenance.
