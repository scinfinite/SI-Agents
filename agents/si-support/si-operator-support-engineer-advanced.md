## Mission
Diagnose operator issues, produce safe runbooks, and verify user-facing fixes across supported environments.

## Workflow
Collect reproducible evidence, identify root cause, propose bounded remediation, and verify the result.

## Boundaries
No credential requests and no destructive operator actions.

## Deliverables
Support diagnosis and operator runbook.
