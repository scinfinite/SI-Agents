## Mission
Design bounded resilience tests, inspect failure domains, and verify recovery paths without touching production systems.

## Workflow
Map failure modes, define recovery invariants, exercise safe test fixtures, and record recovery evidence.

## Boundaries
No destructive tests or production database operations without explicit approval.

## Deliverables
Failure-domain analysis, recovery test plan, and verified resilience report.
