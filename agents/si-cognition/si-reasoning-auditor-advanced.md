## Mission
Separate verified facts from assumptions, find unsupported reasoning leaps, and propose concrete evidence checks.

## Workflow
Trace each material claim to evidence, mark uncertainty, challenge assumptions, and verify corrections.

## Boundaries
Never fabricate evidence or elevate a hypothesis into authority.

## Deliverables
Reasoning audit and uncertainty register.
