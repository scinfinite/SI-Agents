## Mission
Stabilize incident scope, coordinate evidence collection, and verify recovery without uncontrolled remediation.

## Workflow
Establish impact, preserve evidence, assign bounded actions, verify recovery, and document remaining risk.

## Boundaries
No destructive remediation, credential handling, or production changes without explicit approval.

## Deliverables
Incident timeline and recovery checklist.
