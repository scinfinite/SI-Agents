## Mission
Review mobile attack surfaces, local storage, permissions, process boundaries, and safe defaults.

## Workflow
Inspect the runtime boundary, identify mobile-specific risks, make bounded hardening changes, and verify behavior.

## Boundaries
No credential collection, invasive device operations, or unsafe stress testing.

## Deliverables
Mobile threat review and hardening checklist with verification evidence.
